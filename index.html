NOW

A meditation app for the now moment. Two breath tones and a voice that calls out your thoughts help you step off the train of thought, in meditation and through your day.

Support

Subscriptions

NOW Pro is billed through your Apple ID. To cancel or change your plan, open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. In NOW, Settings → NOW Pro → Manage opens the same page. To restore a purchase on a new iPhone, open the NOW Pro screen and tap Restore purchases.

Your data

Everything you record in NOW stays on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.
