NOW PRIVACY POLICY

Effective November 10, 2026

NOW is a meditation app. It is built so that your practice stays with you. NOW does not collect, sell or share your personal information, and it has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you create in NOW is stored only on your device:

  • Your meditation log: the date, start time and length of each session, the breaths and check-ins counted, and any mood or note you add.
  • Hours you enter from before you used NOW.
  • Your settings, such as breath timing, tones, volumes, theme and reminder times.
  • Check-in phrases you record in your own voice.

We never receive this information. If you use iCloud or iTunes device backups, Apple may include it in those backups under Apple’s own privacy terms.

Microphone

NOW asks for microphone access only when you choose to record your own check-in phrase, or when you host a group meditation and choose to speak to your group. Recordings of your phrases are saved on your iPhone and played back during your sessions; they are never uploaded. NOW does not listen at any other time.

Group meditations

Group meditations let people sit together live. To keep a group in step, NOW connects to a small NOW server run on Cloudflare. A group needs no account, no name and no sign-in.

  • What the server holds while a group is open: the group’s code, the settings its host chose (such as breath timing, tones, the start time and an optional group name), and how many people are connected. It keeps a count of how many joined; it does not know who they are.
  • The host’s live voice: if a host speaks to the group, their voice is passed through the server to the people in that group as it happens. It is not recorded or stored. Only the host’s microphone is ever used; people who join only listen.
  • What never leaves your iPhone: your session’s time, heart rate, mood, notes and history. Nobody in a group, including the host, can see anyone else’s session.
  • How long it lasts: a group’s details are deleted when it ends, or about twelve hours after its start time.

As with any internet connection, Cloudflare processes your device’s IP address to deliver it, under Cloudflare’s own privacy terms. NOW does not use it to identify you.

Notifications

If you turn on breath reminders, or join a group meditation scheduled for later, NOW schedules the reminders on your iPhone. They are not sent from a server.

Apple Health and Apple Watch

If you allow it, NOW saves your sessions to Apple Health as Mindful Minutes, and NOW on Apple Watch records your heart rate during meditation as a Mind and Body session. Heart rate readings are sent from your Watch to your iPhone and stored with that session in your Log, only on your device. NOW never uses health data for advertising or marketing, never sells it, never shares it with anyone, and never stores it in iCloud. The heart rate figures and settle score are wellness estimates, not medical measurements. You can change these permissions at any time in the Health app.

Screen Time (doom scroll pause)

If you turn on Doom scroll pause, NOW asks for Screen Time permission through Apple’s Screen Time framework. You choose the apps it watches and how much use comes before a pause. iOS keeps your usage inside Apple’s system: NOW cannot see how long you used any app or which apps you chose, only that the time you set was reached, so it can send the pause notification. Short pauses you take are saved in your Log on your iPhone. You can turn this off in NOW or in the Settings app at any time.

Widgets and the lock screen

The home screen widget and the lock screen session display show your meditation time. They read it from your device and send nothing anywhere.

Subscriptions

NOW Pro is purchased through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles payment and shares no payment details with us. NOW only checks with the App Store whether your subscription is active. You can manage or cancel it in your Apple ID settings.

Children

NOW does not collect personal information from anyone, including children, so it can be used at any age.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual sessions in the Log. Deleting the app removes all of your NOW data from your iPhone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
